# Little Colors Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Harsh Vishwakarma provides Little Colors, an offline coloring and learning app designed for young children. Little Colors does not collect or share personal information from children or adults. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or third-party tracking.

## What stays on the device

Little Colors saves sound and music preferences, drawing mode, and the last selected picture and lessons in the app’s local storage. Drawings stay in memory during play. Little Colors does not send these settings, drawings, or play activity to the developer or to anyone else.

You can delete the saved settings by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling Little Colors.

## Contact

Parents and guardians can ask privacy questions through the [Little Colors GitHub project](https://github.com/harshvishu/LittleColors/issues). GitHub issues are public. Please leave out names, contact details, and information about children when posting.
